Philosophy Consistency

7.5 / 10 Strong

The brand system is clear: both files share the cream, sage, gold, terra palette and the Cormorant/DM Sans/JetBrains roles from DESIGN.md, with matching header, banner, section-label, and course-card vocabulary. Drift appears where the code invents values outside the token set: pure white surfaces at index.html:203 and agenda-2026.html:325, dark gradient values at index.html:341, and pill filters at agenda-2026.html:435 despite the design system banning pill-shaped primary controls.

  • Keep: The contemplative editorial direction and Spanish tone stay coherent across both pages.
  • Fix: Replace invented whites, darks, and pill radii with approved tokens.
  • Quick win: Add --ink-2 from DESIGN.md and remove hard-coded inline colors.

Visual Hierarchy

7.0 / 10 Strong

The landing hierarchy is readable: the hero at index.html:609-650 gives the H1, lede, CTAs, and stat sidebar distinct weight, while the Camino split at index.html:655-718 cleanly separates explanation from progression. The agenda page is more utilitarian and works at the component level, but the 160px placeholder banner at agenda-2026.html:567-575 dominates the first viewport before the actual page task appears, and the tab bar becomes another sticky layer under the nav at agenda-2026.html:178-185.

  • Keep: Serif display headings, mono section labels, and restrained CTAs establish clear reading tiers.
  • Fix: Reduce or visually subordinate the placeholder banner when the page is being evaluated as a live design.
  • Quick win: Give the agenda header a compact summary row so users know what the calendar contains before scrolling.

Detail Execution

5.8 / 10 Functional

Spacing and card structure are mostly tidy, especially the step rows at index.html:667-714 and event cards at agenda-2026.html:352-389. The misses are visible to an expert eye: many component styles are repeated between files instead of shared, inline styles appear in content blocks like index.html:633-640 and agenda-2026.html:961, transition timing often uses ease or linear instead of the prescribed cubic-bezier, and the landing mini-calendar is hand-coded with an incorrect June 2026 offset at index.html:743-747.

  • Keep: The event dot/chip language is precise and easy to scan.
  • Fix: Extract shared CSS and remove inline style patches before this grows into a maintenance problem.
  • Quick win: Correct the mini-calendar preview or render it from the same event data as the agenda page.

Functionality

5.8 / 10 Functional

The agenda calendar is the strongest functional piece: it renders month grids, event counts, selected-day details, filters courses, and initializes from a fixed TODAY value at agenda-2026.html:832-991. But many user-facing commands are inert buttons: Comenzar, Inicia el camino, Explorar cursos, Ver, and Inscribirse do not navigate or submit. Mobile hides the nav at index.html:562 and agenda-2026.html:553 without a replacement, and changeMonth can leave the promised Jan-Dec 2026 scope at agenda-2026.html:889-896.

  • Keep: Calendar day selection and course filtering are useful, concrete interactions.
  • Fix: Convert inert buttons to links or wire them to real flows.
  • Quick win: Disable previous on January and next on December to honor the 2026-only brief.

Innovation

6.0 / 10 Functional

The work is tasteful but conservative. The best distinctive move is the agenda dark band on the landing page at index.html:340-405, which turns the calendar preview into a memorable editorial interruption. The rest relies on familiar premium-site patterns: stat cards, three course cards, sticky nav, tabbed calendar, and marquee. Those are appropriate for the school, but not yet surprising enough to make the design feel unmistakably owned.

  • Keep: The dark agenda band creates contrast without becoming tech-startup dark mode.
  • Fix: Give one core brand idea a custom visual treatment instead of relying on generic cards.
  • Quick win: Replace course thumbnail gradients with quiet symbolic or photographic assets tied to each practice.

Keep

  • The shared banner/header/page rhythm across both files.
  • The Cormorant/DM Sans/JetBrains type hierarchy from the design system.
  • The agenda color taxonomy: sage for meditation, gold for course starts, terra for retreats.
  • The landing page's Camino split layout and agenda dark band.

Fix

  • Wire or replace all inert CTA buttons with real links or handlers.
  • Add a mobile navigation affordance instead of hiding the nav entirely.
  • Clamp agenda month navigation to January through December 2026.
  • Remove token violations: pure white, invented dark gradient colors, and 999px pill filters.
  • Correct the landing mini-calendar's June 2026 layout.

Quick Wins

  • Change button CTAs that navigate into a elements with real destinations.
  • Use the same calendar renderer for the landing preview and agenda page.
  • Swap #fff for an approved cream token or add a documented elevated-surface token.
  • Replace transition: all, ease, and linear with the motion token from DESIGN.md.
  • Remove inline styles from stat cards and course metadata into named classes.
